Example Cards

  2. Download: save the file, then use Load from File.

4. Expert

DOOM

DOOM mods, map packs and the original games, ready to play. Also Heretic, Hexen and Strife.

  1. Look at: catalog (the mod list), source ports, Steam games, 3D Settings, a theme and a font.
  2. This card is a package: it brings two DLLs (a 3D fix for Doomsday, and a 3D crosshair dot for UZDoom, GZDoom and LZDoom) and their source code.
