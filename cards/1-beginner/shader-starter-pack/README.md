# Shader Starter Pack

**Level 1: Beginner.** Six open shader packs for ReShade, by their makers.

## Load it

1. Copy the card's link on the [website](https://blueskydefender.github.io/GPU-Selector-Cards/cards.html).
2. In GPU Selector: **Mods** tab, **+ Add Mod**, **Load from URL**, paste the link.
3. Or download `shader_starter_pack.json` and use **Load from File**.

## What to look at

| Part | Field |
|---|---|
| Shader packs it installs | `shaderRepos` |
| Games with a ReShade preset | `targetGames` with `"match": "preset"` |
| Tabs, one per pack | `ui.modes` |
