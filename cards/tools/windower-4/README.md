# Windower 4

A tool card for [Windower 4](https://www.windower.net) (FINAL FANTASY XI).

1. Finds your Windower 4 folder, or downloads Windower.
2. Backs up your launcher settings and profiles (`settings.xml`) and your init script (`scripts\init.txt`).
3. Lets you move your setup to another computer.

This card used to be built into GPU Selector. It is in Cards on the House now, so it can change without an app update.

Windower 4 keeps its files next to `Windower.exe`, so the card only reads files in the Windower folder. Windower 5 (an alpha) keeps them in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Windower` and `Saved Games\Windower` instead; this card is for Windower 4.
